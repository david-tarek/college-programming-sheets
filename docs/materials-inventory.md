## Source Handling

- The PDFs remain in the local study folder.
- Only reviewed solutions and original documentation are added to the public
  repository.
- A sheet is not marked started or completed until its questions have been
  reviewed.
- Missing or conflicting materials are recorded instead of being guessed.
