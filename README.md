# College Programming Sheets — C++ Solutions

A structured collection of C++ solutions, explanations, and study resources for **COMP 205 — Object-Oriented Programming** at the Faculty of Science, Ain Shams University.

The repository is organized by academic cohort, sheet, and question to make learning, practice, and exam revision easier.

## 🎯 Purpose

- Organize programming sheets by academic year.
- Provide clean, readable C++ solutions for every question.
- Explain problem-solving approaches step by step.
- Include relevant video tutorials and learning resources.
- Track sheet completion and solution progress.
- Build a reliable reference for midterm and final exam revision.

## 📄 What Does Each Sheet Contain?

Every sheet has its own dedicated folder containing:

- **README.md:** Sheet overview, problem descriptions, step-by-step explanations, examples, and recommended video tutorials or playlists.
- **C++ Solution Files:** A separate `.cpp` file for each question, containing its corresponding solution.
- **Video Resources:** Curated links documented in the sheet's README, matched to the concepts and problems covered.

Each cohort also has its own `README.md` to list its available sheets and track their progress.

The structure above is illustrative. Actual files and folders are added according to the available course materials and completed work.

## 🎥 Learning Resources

Relevant video tutorials and playlists are documented inside each sheet's README. Resources are selected according to the concepts required by the questions, helping students understand both the theory and practical implementation.

## ✅ Progress Tracking

Sheets follow three stages:

- **Inventoried:** The sheet has been identified and recorded.
- **In Progress:** Solutions, testing, or documentation are underway.
- **Completed:** All questions have been solved, tested, and documented.

A sheet is marked as completed only after its solutions and documentation have been reviewed.
